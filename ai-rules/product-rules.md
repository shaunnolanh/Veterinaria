# Product Design Principles

The websites generated must follow strict usability principles.

---

# Target Market

Small local businesses in small towns.

Examples:

- veterinarias
- restaurantes
- ferreterías
- corralones
- tiendas de barrio

---

# Design Philosophy

The design must prioritize:

- clarity
- readability
- simplicity
- fast loading
- mobile usability

---

# Mandatory Website Sections

Most websites should include:

Home  
Products / Services  
About the business  
Location / Map  
Contact  
Opening hours

---

# Admin Panel Requirements

Each website should include an admin panel allowing the business owner to:

- update products
- modify prices
- update photos
- change opening hours
- update contact information

The admin panel must be extremely simple.

---

# Accessibility

Design must work well for users who:

- are not tech-savvy
- use mobile phones primarily
- may have limited digital literacy
