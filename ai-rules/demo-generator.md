# Demo Website Generator

Claude must support the generation of demo websites for commercial presentation.

These demo websites allow the company to show realistic examples to potential clients before selling a full website service.

---

# Purpose

Demo websites should simulate a real business website for presentation purposes.

The objective is to quickly generate visually complete examples that can be customized later.

---

# Supported Demo Types

Claude should be able to generate demos for common local businesses such as:

- veterinaria
- restaurante
- ferretería
- corralón
- tienda de barrio
- panadería
- peluquería
- farmacia

---

# Demo Structure

Each demo website should include:

Home page  
Services or products  
About the business  
Location map  
Contact form  
Opening hours

---

# Demo Content Strategy

Claude should generate placeholder content that looks realistic.

Example:

Veterinaria San Martín  
"Más de 20 años cuidando a tus mascotas"

Services:
- vacunación
- consultas
- cirugía veterinaria
- peluquería canina

---

# Demo Visual Design

Demo websites should:

- look professional
- be mobile friendly
- include sample images
- include sample products or services

---

# Demo Naming Convention

Examples:

demo-veterinaria-central  
demo-restaurante-plaza  
demo-ferreteria-norte  

---

# Customization Phase

Once a customer purchases the website service, the demo should be easily convertible into a production website by replacing demo content with real business data.
